What's Inside

Everything a two-sided marketplace needs — nothing it doesn't.

Six capabilities that ship in the app, not a wishlist.

Phone-OTP Authentication

Every account is verified by a real SMS one-time code — no passwords to leak or forget.

Real GPS Discovery

Every listing is pinned at its real location. Browse the map or filter by category and price.

Direct WhatsApp Contact

Reach out to a host or specialist directly on WhatsApp before or after a booking request.

Flexible Booking Requests

Hourly, shift-based, daily or monthly — hosts set real availability, specialists request and confirm.

Admin-Governed Trust

Disputes and account governance are handled by a human Admin console — every action is audit-logged.

Lebanon-Ready, as a Plus

Terms and data handling are already drafted around Lebanese Law No. 81/2018 — one less thing to adapt.

Three Steps

From open app to confirmed booking.

The same simple flow, whichever side you're on.

01

Sign in with your phone

One SMS code and a short profile — specialty, city, and (for hosts) your first listing.

02

Browse or publish

Specialists scan the map for the right room; hosts set pricing, photos and real availability.

03

Request, confirm, meet

A booking request lands in the host's inbox; once accepted, the calendar locks and WhatsApp takes it from there.
