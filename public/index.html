FAQ

Questions from space owners

How does the 50% launch offer work?

New ProHost Premium subscribers pay half price for their first subscription period, on the monthly or the yearly plan. Google Play shows the exact discounted price before you confirm, and the regular price applies from the next renewal. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Does ProHost take a commission on my bookings?

No. ProHost currently doesn't charge booking fees or process payments between you and the professional; you agree and settle payment directly. Premium is a flat subscription, so what you earn from your bookings is yours.

Who can book my space?

Professionals with a verified phone number and a profile photo. You see each request before it's confirmed, and you accept or decline it.

Can I list several rooms with different prices?

Yes. Add every room in your space, each with its own way of renting (hour, shift, day, month or per attendee) and its own prices.

What happens if I change my prices?

Choose whether the change applies now or from the next term, booking by booking. ProHost notifies the affected tenants for you.

Is ProHost available on iPhone?

ProHost is on Android (Google Play) today. Leave your email below and we'll let you know when the iPhone app is ready.
