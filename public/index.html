What's Inside

Everything a two-sided marketplace needs — nothing it doesn't.

Six capabilities that actually ship in the app, not a wishlist.

Phone-OTP Authentication

Every account is verified by a real SMS one-time code through Firebase Authentication — no passwords to leak or forget.

Real GPS Discovery

Every listing is pinned at its real location. Browse the interactive map or filter by governorate, category and price.

Direct WhatsApp Contact

Skip the middleman — reach out to a host or specialist directly on WhatsApp before or after a booking request.

Flexible Booking Requests

Hourly, shift-based, daily or monthly — a host sets real availability and pricing, a specialist requests and gets confirmed.

Admin-Governed Trust

Suspicious activity, disputes and account governance are handled by a real Admin console — every action is audit-logged.

Built on Lebanese Law

Data handling and platform terms are drafted around Law No. 81/2018 and the practical realities of the Lebanese market.

Three Steps

From open app to confirmed booking.

The same simple flow, whichever side you're on.

01

Sign in with your phone

One SMS code and a short profile — specialty, city, and (for hosts) your first listing.

02

Browse or publish

Specialists scan the map for the right room; hosts set pricing, photos and real availability.

03

Request, confirm, meet

A booking request lands in the host's inbox; once accepted, the calendar locks and WhatsApp takes it from there.
