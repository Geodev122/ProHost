Delete Your Account

Application: ProHost

You can delete your ProHost account and the personal data associated with it at any time, either directly in the app or by requesting deletion here if you no longer have access to it.

1. Delete In-App (Immediate)

The fastest way to delete your account:

  • Open the ProHost app and sign in.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Scroll to the danger-zone section and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This is processed immediately and permanently.

2. Request Deletion Without App Access

If you can't sign in to the app anymore, email us at geo.elnajjar@gmail.com from the address on file, or include the phone number your account is registered under, and ask us to delete your account. We process these requests within 30 days.

3. What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, specialty, city, profile picture).
  • Your uploaded ID document and, for Pro Hosts, ownership/verification documents.
  • Every workspace listing you own, including its photos.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account itself — you won't be able to sign back in.

What we keep: booking records that involve another user (a specialist's booking against your listing, or a listing you booked as a specialist) are kept as that other party's transaction history, the same way a receipt isn't erased from the other side of a transaction. These records are not linked back to a deleted account's personal profile.
