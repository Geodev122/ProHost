FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep the same therapy room every week?

Yes. Book a weekly pattern, for example Tuesdays 16:00 to 19:00, and once the host accepts, those slots are reserved for you on every date of your term.

Are therapy rooms private and soundproofed?

Each listing describes the room and its facilities. Many hosts are therapy and wellness practices with rooms designed for confidential sessions. Ask the host on WhatsApp before you book if soundproofing matters for your work.

Do my clients need the app?

No. Only you book the room. Your clients simply come to the address of the practice at the time you booked.

Is it free to book a room?

Yes. Professionals search and book on ProHost for free; you pay the host directly for the room.
