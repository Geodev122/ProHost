FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to list my space?

Publishing and managing listings is part of ProHost Premium, a monthly or yearly subscription billed by Google Play. New subscribers get their first month free on the monthly plan, or 50% off their first year on the yearly plan, and the exact price and billing start date are shown in Google Play before you confirm.

Does ProHost take a commission on bookings?

No. ProHost doesn't charge booking fees or process payments between you and the professional. You agree and settle payment directly.

Can I list several rooms with different prices?

Yes. Add every room in your space, each with its own pricing method, schedule and photos.

Can I decline a request?

Yes. You see every request before it is confirmed and you accept or decline it, optionally with a reason.
