Why ProHost

Book exactly the time you need

Pay for your hours, not a lease

Clinic space is often rented by the year. On ProHost you book a morning shift twice a week, a full day on Saturdays or a month at a time.

Rooms inside real medical centers

Hosts are medical centers, polyclinics and doctors with rooms that sit empty part of the week, with reception and waiting areas already in place.

Live availability per room

How it works

From search to confirmed booking in three steps

  1. Search the map

    Open ProHost, filter by space type, room and pricing formula, and see what is available in your area.

  2. Pick your slots

    Open a room, check its live availability and choose the days and hours you need. The total is shown before you send.

  3. Get confirmed

    The host accepts your request in the app. Track it in My Rentals, message the host on WhatsApp and add payment reminders.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent a clinic just for a few hours a week?

Yes. Many clinic rooms on ProHost are offered by the shift or by the hour, so you can book, for example, Monday and Thursday mornings only. Each booking request shows the exact dates and the total before you send it.

What is usually included in a clinic room?

Each listing shows its own facilities and utilities, such as an examination bed, Wi-Fi, a generator, reception or a waiting area. Ask the host on WhatsApp from the listing if you need specific equipment.

How do I pay for the clinic?

You agree and settle payment directly with the host. ProHost doesn't charge professionals any booking fee, and you can add payment reminders to your calendar from My Rentals.

How do I find a clinic room near me?

Every listing appears on a live map. Open the map in the app, search your area and filter by space type and pricing formula to see which clinic rooms are available today. Availability changes daily.
