FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance can I book a meeting room?

You can request any open date in the room's calendar. Hosts usually confirm in the app, and you can message them on WhatsApp if your meeting is urgent.

Can I book a hotel conference room for a training?

Yes. Hotels list their meeting and conference rooms on ProHost, often priced per attendee for workshops and trainings. Enter your number of attendees to see the total.

How do I find a meeting room near me?

Open the map in the app, search your area and filter by space type and pricing formula. Each room shows its capacity and live availability. Availability changes daily.

Is there a booking fee?

No. Booking on ProHost is free for professionals. You pay the host the room price agreed in the request.
