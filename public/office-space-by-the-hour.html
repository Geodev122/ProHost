FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent an office just for one day?

Yes. Offices offered by the day or by the hour can be booked for a single date. Monthly offices can be rented month by month.

What is the difference between a desk and a private office?

A desk is a seat in a shared area, usually in a coworking space. A private office is a closed room for you or your team. Each listing shows which it is.

Do offices have backup power?

Many hosts list a generator or UPS in their facilities. Check the listing or ask the host on WhatsApp.

How do I find an office near me?

Every listing appears on a live map. Open the map in the app, search your area and filter by space type and pricing formula to see what is available today.
