Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Last updated: 26 September 2026

Summary: ProHost is a marketplace platform. We collect only what we need to connect specialists with host spaces. We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers. You can access, correct, export or delete your account and all associated data at any time, wherever in the world you use ProHost.

1. Who We Are

ProHost ("we", "us", "our") operates a mobile and web platform that connects independent professionals ("Specialists") with property owners or operators ("Pro Hosts") who list rentable clinic, office and workspace subdivisions. We act as a platform intermediary and are not a party to any rental agreement between users. ProHost is the data controller (or, for California and similar U.S. state laws, the "business") responsible for the personal data described in this Policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Phone number — used to verify your identity via one-time SMS code (Firebase Authentication). We do not store passwords.
  • Display name and specialty/profession — provided during registration; shown to the other party in a booking transaction.
  • Profile photo — optional; stored in Google Cloud Storage if uploaded.
  • Government-issued ID document — collected only if and when you complete identity verification ("KYC") in order to become a Pro Host; never requested at registration, and never required of a Specialist. It is reviewed by a ProHost Administrator before your account is approved as a Pro Host.

2.2 Listing and Booking Data

  • Space address, GPS coordinates, photos, pricing and availability configured by Pro Hosts.
  • Booking requests, confirmation status and rental period.
  • WhatsApp phone number shared at the point of confirmed booking (Specialists see host's number and vice versa).

2.3 Usage and Device Data

  • Approximate location (device GPS), used only while the app is open to show nearby spaces. Not stored server-side continuously.
  • Crash logs and performance metrics via Google Firebase Crashlytics and Performance — anonymised and aggregated.
  • App usage events (screens opened, search terms) via Firebase Analytics — aggregated and not linked to individual identity without consent.

2.4 Communications

ProHost does not host or store chat messages. Direct communication happens on WhatsApp, which is governed by Meta's own privacy policy. We only store the event record (booking request/acceptance) within our platform.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To verify identity and prevent fraud (SMS OTP, and ID verification for Pro Hosts only).
  • To display your listing to users searching in the app.
  • To facilitate booking requests and confirmations.
  • To provide customer support and investigate disputes.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including data-protection, consumer-protection, and tax/accounting laws applicable in the jurisdictions where we or our users operate.
  • To improve the platform through aggregated analytics — never sold to third parties.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR"), the UK GDPR, or an equivalent law applies to our processing of your personal data, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing necessary to register your account and fulfil booking transactions you initiate.
  • Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — fraud prevention, ID verification for Pro Hosts, platform security, and aggregated analytics, balanced against your rights and never overriding them.
  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) — optional analytics and marketing communications; you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)) — compliance with applicable law, including responding to a lawful request from a competent authority.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as that term is used in the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"). We share data only as follows:

  • Between users — a Specialist's name, specialty and phone number are shared with a Pro Host upon a confirmed booking, and vice versa.
  • Google / Firebase — Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Functions, Crashlytics and Analytics are operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited as our service provider / processor under appropriate data processing terms. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Legal process — we may disclose data if required by a valid court order, legal obligation or to protect the rights and safety of users.
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger, acquisition or asset sale, data may transfer subject to the same privacy commitments described here, and you will be notified of any change in controller.

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained while your account remains open.
  • Booking records are retained for five (5) years for legal and dispute-resolution purposes, or longer where a longer statutory limitation period applies in the relevant jurisdiction.
  • Upon account deletion, personal data is purged within thirty (30) calendar days except where retention is required by law.
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Your specific rights depend on where you live. We honour the applicable rights below for every user, regardless of location, as a matter of policy — not only where strictly required by law.

7.1 If You Are in the EEA, UK, or a Similar GDPR-Aligned Jurisdiction

  • Access (Art. 15) a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) of your account and associated personal data — available directly in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by written request to admin@pro-host.tech or via our web request form. Processed within 30 days.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21) in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability (Art. 20) — a machine-readable export of data you provided.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) for optional analytics or communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority — see Section 12.

7.2 If You Are a California or Other U.S. State Resident

Under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws (for example Virginia's VCDPA, Colorado's CPA, and Connecticut's CTDPA), you may have the right to:

  • Know / Access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you and the purposes for collection, as described in Section 2 and Section 3.
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions (e.g. completing a transaction, legal compliance).
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of "sale" or "sharing" — we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of; we do not use dark patterns to obscure this fact.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information — we do not use your government ID document or precise geolocation for any purpose beyond identity verification, safety, and providing the core booking/discovery features you request.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny you goods or services, or provide a different level of service, because you exercised a privacy right.
  • Appeal a denied request, and designate an authorised agent to submit a request on your behalf (subject to identity verification).

To exercise any right in Section 7.1 or 7.2, contact us at admin@pro-host.tech. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally thirty (30) to forty-five (45) days).

8. Security

We employ industry-standard security measures: HTTPS-only data transmission, Firebase Security Rules limiting data access to authenticated owners, server-side-only enforcement of sensitive fields (role, verification status, payment entitlements), and no storage of passwords or payment card data. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach creating a real risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and, where legally required, the competent supervisory authority, without undue delay and in any event within the timeframe required by applicable law (for example 72 hours under GDPR Art. 33 where feasible).

9. Cookies and Tracking on This Website

The ProHost marketing website (pro-host.tech) uses no third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers; see Google's Privacy Policy for how font requests are handled. We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal and equivalent "Do Not Track" browser signals as a valid opt-out request under CCPA/CPRA where applicable, though as noted in Section 5 we do not sell or share personal information in any event.

10. Children's Privacy

ProHost is not directed at persons under 18 years of age (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete the account and associated data promptly.
