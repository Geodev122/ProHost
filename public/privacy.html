Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Last updated: 1 June 2025

Summary: ProHost is a marketplace platform. We collect only what we need to connect specialists with host spaces. We do not sell or broker your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

1. Who We Are

ProHost ("we", "us", "our") operates a mobile and web platform that connects independent professionals ("Specialists") with property owners or operators ("Pro Hosts") who list rentable clinic, office and workspace subdivisions. We act as a platform intermediary and are not a party to any rental agreement between users.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Phone number — used to verify your identity via one-time SMS code (Firebase Authentication). We do not store passwords.
  • Display name and specialty/profession — provided during registration; shown to the other party in a booking transaction.
  • Profile photo — optional; stored in Google Cloud Storage if uploaded.

2.2 Listing and Booking Data

  • Space address, GPS coordinates, photos, pricing and availability configured by Pro Hosts.
  • Booking requests, confirmation status and rental period.
  • WhatsApp phone number shared at the point of confirmed booking (Specialists see host's number and vice versa).

2.3 Usage and Device Data

  • Approximate location (device GPS), used only while the app is open to show nearby spaces. Not stored server-side continuously.
  • Crash logs and performance metrics via Google Firebase Crashlytics and Performance — anonymised and aggregated.
  • App usage events (screens opened, search terms) via Firebase Analytics — aggregated and not linked to individual identity without consent.

2.4 Communications

ProHost does not host or store chat messages. Direct communication happens on WhatsApp, which is governed by Meta's own privacy policy. We only store the event record (booking request/acceptance) within our platform.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To verify identity and prevent fraud (SMS OTP).
  • To display your listing to users searching in the app.
  • To facilitate booking requests and confirmations.
  • To provide customer support and investigate disputes.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including Law No. 81/2018 (Lebanon) on consumer protection and applicable data protection regulations.
  • To improve the platform through aggregated analytics — never sold to third parties.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable under law, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to register your account and fulfil booking transactions you initiate.
  • Legitimate interest — fraud prevention, platform security, and aggregated analytics.
  • Consent — optional analytics and marketing communications; you may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — compliance with Lebanese and applicable international law.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only as follows:

  • Between users — a Specialist's name, specialty and phone number are shared with a Pro Host upon a confirmed booking, and vice versa.
  • Google / Firebase — Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Functions, Crashlytics and Analytics are operated by Google LLC. Google acts as a data processor under appropriate data processing agreements. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Legal process — we may disclose data if required by a valid court order, legal obligation or to protect the rights and safety of users.
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger, acquisition or asset sale, data may transfer subject to the same privacy commitments.

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained while your account remains open.
  • Booking records are retained for five (5) years for legal and dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Upon account deletion, personal data is purged within thirty (30) calendar days except where retention is required by law.
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data — available directly in the app (Settings → Delete Account) or by submitting a written request to admin@pro-host.tech or via our web request form. Deletion is processed within 30 days.
  • Object or restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability — request a machine-readable export of data you provided.
  • Withdraw consent for optional analytics or communications at any time.

To exercise any right, contact us at admin@pro-host.tech. We will respond within thirty (30) days.

8. Security

We employ industry-standard security measures: HTTPS-only data transmission, Firebase Security Rules limiting data access to authenticated owners, and no storage of passwords or payment card data. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security and you use the platform at your own risk.

9. Cookies and Tracking on This Website

The ProHost marketing website (pro-host.tech) uses no third-party tracking cookies. Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers; see Google's Privacy Policy for how font requests are handled. No advertising, affiliate or behavioural tracking scripts are loaded.

10. Children's Privacy

ProHost is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete the account promptly.

11. Limitation of Liability and Disclaimer

We are not a party to any rental agreement, employment arrangement, or professional services contract entered into between Specialists and Pro Hosts through the platform. ProHost makes no warranty as to the accuracy of listings, the fitness of a space for a particular professional use, or the conduct of any user.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, ProHost's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with the collection or use of personal data shall not exceed the total fees paid by you (if any) to ProHost in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

We are not liable for: (a) any consequential, indirect, incidental or punitive damages; (b) any loss of data resulting from the actions of third-party service providers such as Google Firebase; or (c) any breach resulting from force majeure events.

12. Lebanese Law Compliance

We operate in compliance with Lebanese Law No. 81/2018 (Consumer Protection) and applicable data-protection obligations. Users located in Lebanon have all rights described in Section 7. We do not transfer personal data to jurisdictions without adequate data protection safeguards without appropriate contractual safeguards in place.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy at any time. We will notify registered users of material changes via in-app notification at least fourteen (14) days before they take effect. Continued use of the platform after that date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions or Requests
