Privacy Policy

Application: ProHost | Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Welcome to ProHost ("we", "our", or "us"). ProHost is a professional workspace and practice exchange platform connecting independent specialists (physicians, therapists, architects, consultants) with workspace hosts renting medical clinics, consulting offices, and professional studios.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the ProHost mobile application, our website (https://prohost-f766f.web.app and https://pro-host.tech), and related cloud services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our co-sharing exchange services, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number (verified via Firebase Authentication SMS One-Time Passcode), Profession/Job Title, City, and optional Profile Picture.
  • Identity Verification Documents: National ID, Passport, or professional license documents uploaded during profile setup or host verification to maintain platform safety.
  • Location Data: Precise (GPS) and approximate location data when using the interactive map to discover nearby workspaces or pin listing locations.
  • Listing & Rental Data: Workspace titles, photos, descriptions, operating schedules, subdivision setups, pricing rules, and rental booking request history.
  • Financial & Payment Transaction Info: Whish Money transaction identifiers, order amounts, and rental package subscription statuses. We do not store raw credit card numbers or private payment PINs.
  • Device & Technical Usage Data: Device model, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens, IP address, and security audit log entries.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following specific purposes:

  • To authenticate your phone number securely via Firebase SMS verification.
  • To display nearby workspace listings on our OpenStreetMap discovery canvas.
  • To facilitate rental booking requests between specialists and workspace hosts.
  • To enable direct WhatsApp communication between confirmed hosts and specialists.
  • To verify host identity and workspace ownership for platform security.
  • To send real-time push notifications regarding booking updates, approvals, and payment reminders.
  • To prevent fraud, enforce platform rules, and comply with applicable laws (including Lebanese Data Protection Law No. 81/2018).

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers. We share information only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the app:

  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase: Cloud database (Firestore), authentication, file storage, and serverless Cloud Functions hosted in Google Cloud's European region.
  • Google Play Integrity API: Used to verify device genuineness and protect against fraud.
  • Whish Money Gateway: Processed strictly for payment transaction verification and settlement.

4. Data Protection & Retention

Your data is protected using SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest within Google Cloud infrastructure. Access to administrative controls is restricted through server-verified Firebase Auth custom claims and audit logging.

5. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You have full control over your data and can request deletion at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Profile → Delete Account inside the app for immediate automated deletion of your profile, listings, uploaded ID documents, and Firebase Auth account.
  • Web Request: Visit Delete Account Page or email us at geo.elnajjar@gmail.com to request manual account deletion within 30 days.
