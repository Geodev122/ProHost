Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | Application: ProHost

ProHost ("the Platform", "we", "us", "our") is a marketplace platform connecting independent healthcare practitioners and professional specialists ("Specialists") with workspace hosts ("Pro Hosts") in Lebanon. This Privacy Policy details how ProHost collects, uses, stores, and safeguards personal data when you use the ProHost mobile application and associated web services.

1. Regulatory Framework & Governance

ProHost respects personal privacy and complies with Lebanese Law No. 81/2018 on Electronic Transactions and Personal Data. Where applicable, our data management practices align with international privacy standards including the principles of purpose limitation, data minimization, and secure storage.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver core marketplace features and verify account authenticity, ProHost collects the following categories of information:

  • Phone Number & Authentication Data: Your mobile phone number is collected during sign-in and verified via a One-Time Passcode (OTP) sent through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile & Professional Information: Full name, professional job title/specialty, email address, city, governorate, and optional profile picture.
  • Verification Documents: National ID or passport documents uploaded for identity verification and fraud prevention.
  • Location & GPS Data: Fine and coarse device location (when permitted) to auto-detect country dial codes and show nearby rental workspaces on interactive maps.
  • Workspace Listing Data: Workspace titles, descriptions, address details, equipment inventories, and hourly/monthly rental pricing submitted by Pro Hosts.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate marketplace operation purposes:

  • Authenticating your account via SMS OTP without requiring passwords.
  • Facilitating workspace discovery, booking coordination, and host-specialist inquiries.
  • Verifying member identities to maintain platform safety and trust.
  • Sending transactional status alerts, booking confirmations, and push notifications.
  • Complying with legal obligations under Lebanese law.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

ProHost does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. We share data only with essential cloud infrastructure providers operating under strict confidentiality:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Provides secure user authentication (Phone Auth), encrypted cloud database storage (Firestore), and media storage (Firebase Storage).
  • Google Maps SDK: Powers location display and workspace search mapping.
  • Whish Money: Processes rental subscription fee transactions through encrypted payment gateways.

5. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the ProHost mobile application and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 encryption. User profile media and documents are stored in private Firebase Storage buckets protected by server-side security rules.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

Under Lebanese Law No. 81/2018, you hold the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal profile data. You can update your profile directly within the app or request full account deletion by contacting our privacy officer at geo.elnajjar@gmail.com.
