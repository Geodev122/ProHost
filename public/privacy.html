Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Applies to the ProHost apps for Android and iOS and to pro-host.tech

Summary: ProHost is a marketplace that connects specialists with host spaces. We collect only what we need to run it, we do not sell your personal data, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers. You can access, correct, export or delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email, wherever you live.

What changed on 8 November 2026: we corrected how sign-in works (email and password, Google, and Sign in with Apple; no SMS codes), added the ProHost app for iOS and Apple App Store subscriptions, named the service providers we use (including Google Maps, Apple, our AI assistant provider and our website host), described our automated assistant on Instagram and WhatsApp, and added backup, deletion and EU Digital Services Act details. These changes apply to new accounts, including iOS accounts, from today and to existing users from 22 November 2026 (14 days' notice, see Section 13).

1. Who We Are

ProHost ("we", "us", "our") operates a mobile and web platform (the Android app, the iOS app and pro-host.tech) that connects independent professionals ("Specialists") with property owners or operators ("Pro Hosts") who list rentable clinic, office and workspace subdivisions. We act as a platform intermediary and are not a party to any rental agreement between users. ProHost is the data controller (or, for California and similar U.S. state laws, the "business") responsible for the personal data described in this Policy.

Privacy contact and single point of contact for users and authorities (including under the EU Digital Services Act): admin@pro-host.tech. We write to you in English; you may write to us in English or Arabic.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Email address and sign-in method — you sign in with email and password, with your Google account, or (on iOS) with Sign in with Apple. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive and use Apple's private-relay address as your email. We never see or store your plain password: Firebase Authentication keeps only a salted hash. Email is also used for service emails (verification, password reset, booking and subscription notices).
  • Display name and specialty/profession — provided during registration; shown to the other party in a booking transaction.
  • WhatsApp number — entered by you (it is self-declared and not verified by SMS) so that hosts and specialists can reach you about bookings. On Android you may choose to fill it from the system number picker; we do not read your number without that choice.
  • Country and city — needed to publish a listing and to show relevant spaces.
  • Profile photo — optional; stored in Google Cloud Storage if uploaded.
  • Listing verification documents — only if, as a Pro Host, you ask for the optional "Listing Verified" badge and upload a proof of ownership or of your right to rent out that space; never requested at registration and never required of a Specialist. Documents are stored privately and reviewed by a ProHost administrator.
  • Consent record — the date and version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted.

2.2 Listing and Booking Data

  • Space address, map position (the pin a host places), photos, pricing and availability configured by Pro Hosts.
  • Booking requests, confirmation status and rental period.
  • The WhatsApp number of the other party is shown when a booking request is made or confirmed, so you can arrange the rental directly.
  • Saved listings (favourites), so you can find them again.

2.3 Usage, Device and Location Data

  • Approximate location — used only while the app is open, to show nearby spaces and to detect your country at sign-up.
  • Precise location — requested only when you tap "Locate me" to place or find a workspace on the map; it is used once for that action and is not stored continuously. You may decline, or grant approximate location only, and still use the app (you can place the pin by hand). On iOS and Android the system permission prompt applies and you can change it at any time in system settings.
  • Maps — maps are drawn by the Google Maps SDK. Google receives the information needed to show a map (for example your IP address, device and app details, and the area shown) under Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms.
  • Photos and camera — the app does not request access to your photo library or camera in advance. Photos you add come from the system photo picker (only the photos you select are shared with the app); the camera is used only if you choose "Take photo".
  • Push notifications — if you allow them, we store a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android; Apple Push Notification service through Firebase on iOS) and keep a short in-app notification history (48 hours).
  • Security and device integrity — to protect accounts and our servers from abuse we use Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android, Apple App Attest on iOS). These checks confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of our app; they do not identify you or read your files.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs via Google Firebase Crashlytics — anonymised and never linked to your account.
  • Usage analytics (only if you choose "Allow") via Google Analytics for Firebase: screens opened, searches and filters used, listings and rooms viewed, booking and subscription steps, and errors. These events are linked to your pseudonymous ProHost code (U-…), your role, country, plan status and account-age range — never to your name, email, phone number or an advertising identifier. Some subscription and booking events (for example renewals) are sent from our servers on your behalf, only while your choice is "Allow". Nothing is collected until you choose, and you can change your choice any time in Profile › Privacy. Analytics data is never used for advertising.
  • No tracking. We do not use the advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), we do not combine your data with other companies' data to track you across apps and websites, and therefore we do not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

2.4 Subscription and Purchase Data

If you subscribe to ProHost Premium, the store you bought it from processes the payment: Google Play on Android, the Apple App Store on iOS. We never receive your card or bank details. We receive and store the purchase identifiers (on Google Play the purchase token and order ID; on the App Store the transaction identifiers), the plan (monthly or yearly), the subscription status and the renewal/expiry dates, linked to your account, to verify the purchase, grant and remove Pro Host access and prevent fraud. Both stores send us notifications when a subscription renews, is cancelled or refunded.

2.5 Communications and Our Automated Assistant

  • Between users — ProHost does not host or store chat messages between users. Direct communication happens on WhatsApp, which is governed by Meta's own privacy policy. We only store the event record (booking request/acceptance) within our platform.
  • Our assistant on Instagram and WhatsApp — when you message or comment on ProHost's Instagram account (@prohost.app) or its WhatsApp number, an automated AI assistant may reply. It says that it is an AI assistant, and you can write STOP at any time or ask for a person. We process the messages you send, your public profile name on that platform, and a short summary needed to keep the conversation going, using Anthropic's API as our AI provider (Anthropic does not use this content to train its models under its commercial terms). The assistant only looks up your ProHost account after you give it your registration email and agree, and it never reads back account details. If you choose to leave your name, email, WhatsApp number, profession and city as an office-owner or partner enquiry, we store them as a lead after you consent. Conversations are kept so that a chat can continue and are deleted when you ask us to (write STOP or email us); Meta processes the messages on its platforms under its own policies.
  • Reports and support — if you report content or contact us, we keep the message, the content concerned and our decision.

2.6 Website Contact Form and Newsletter

When you use the contact form on pro-host.tech we store your name, email and, if you give them, your phone number, organization, role and message, and we email your request to our team at admin@pro-host.tech so we can answer you.

If you tick "Send me ProHost news and offers" or subscribe to the newsletter, we keep your email (and the details you gave) in our mailing list and send you occasional ProHost news and offers from noreply@pro-host.tech. Every email has an Unsubscribe link that removes you immediately; you can also write to admin@pro-host.tech.

This data is stored in Google Firebase, is accessible only to ProHost administrators, and is never sold. We keep contact requests for as long as needed to handle them and our relationship with you, and newsletter data until you unsubscribe (after which we keep only a record that you opted out, so we don't email you again).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and prevent fraud and abuse (including attestation checks and, where you request it, listing verification).
  • To display your listing and profile details to users searching in the app.
  • To facilitate booking requests, confirmations and price-change notices.
  • To sell, verify and manage ProHost Premium through Google Play and the Apple App Store.
  • To send service messages and push notifications you need (bookings, subscription changes, security).
  • To provide customer support, handle reports and investigate disputes.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including data-protection, consumer-protection, and tax/accounting laws applicable in the jurisdictions where we or our users operate.
  • To improve the platform through aggregated analytics (only with your consent) and crash diagnostics — never sold to third parties.
  • To answer website contact requests and AI-assistant conversations and, only with your consent, to send ProHost news and offers (see 2.5 and 2.6).

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR"), the UK GDPR, or an equivalent law applies to our processing of your personal data, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing necessary to register your account, provide Premium and fulfil booking transactions you initiate.
  • Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — fraud prevention, device-integrity checks, listing verification, platform security, content moderation and diagnostics, balanced against your rights and never overriding them.
  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) — optional analytics, precise location when you tap "Locate me", push notifications, AI-assistant account look-ups and lead capture, and marketing communications; you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)) — compliance with applicable law, including responding to a lawful request from a competent authority.

5. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as that term is used in the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"). We share data only as follows:

  • Between users — a Specialist's name, specialty and WhatsApp number are shared with a Pro Host when a booking request is made or confirmed, and vice versa.
  • Google (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) — Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Analytics; the Google Maps SDK; and sign-in with Google. Google acts as our service provider / processor under its data processing terms, and for Google sign-in and Google Maps under its own privacy policy. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Play — subscription purchases on Android are processed by Google under the Google Play Terms of Service; we exchange the purchase token with Google's Play Developer API to verify and manage your subscription.
  • Apple (Apple Inc. and Apple Distribution International) — Sign in with Apple, App Store subscription processing and server notifications, Apple Push Notification service, App Attest and TestFlight (for testers). Apple's own Privacy Policy applies to what Apple collects.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic PBC) — AI model provider for our automated assistant (see 2.5). We send only the conversation needed to answer.
  • Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms, Inc.) — Instagram and WhatsApp, through which our assistant and WhatsApp conversations run; Meta's own policies apply.
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc.) — hosts the pro-host.tech website and serves its pages; it processes technical request data such as IP address and requested page for delivery and security.
  • Email delivery and domain services — providers that send our service emails and host our domain and mailboxes, acting only on our instructions.
  • Legal process — we may disclose data if required by a valid court order or legal obligation, or to protect the rights and safety of users.
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger, acquisition or asset sale, data may transfer subject to the same privacy commitments described here, and you will be notified of any change in controller.

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained while your account remains open.
  • Booking records are retained for five (5) years for legal and dispute-resolution purposes, or longer where a longer statutory limitation period applies in the relevant jurisdiction.
  • Subscription and purchase records are kept while the subscription is active and for up to five (5) years after it ends, for tax, accounting and chargeback/fraud purposes.
  • Upon account deletion, personal data is removed from our live systems within thirty (30) calendar days except where retention is required by law. Encrypted system backups (daily backups kept 7 days, weekly backups kept 8 weeks) roll off automatically, so deleted data can remain in backups for up to 8 weeks and is not restored except for disaster recovery.
  • Reports and moderation records are kept for as long as needed to handle the report and protect users, and longer only where a dispute or legal claim requires it.
  • Assistant conversations and leads are kept until you ask us to delete them or, for leads, until the enquiry is completed; STOP or an email to admin@pro-host.tech deletes them.
  • Pseudonymous analytics events are kept for 14 months in Google Analytics and for up to 26 months in our private BigQuery export; aggregated, non-identifiable reports may be retained indefinitely. Withdrawing consent stops collection and resets the analytics identifier on your device.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Your specific rights depend on where you live. We honour the applicable rights below for every user, regardless of location, as a matter of policy — not only where strictly required by law.

7.1 If You Are in the EEA, UK, or a Similar GDPR-Aligned Jurisdiction

  • Access (Art. 15) a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) of inaccurate or incomplete data — most details can be edited in Profile.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) of your account and associated personal data — available directly in the app (Profile → Delete Account, on Android and iOS) or by written request to admin@pro-host.tech or via our web request page. Processed within 30 days. On iOS, deleting your account also revokes the Sign in with Apple connection.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21) in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability (Art. 20) — a machine-readable export of data you provided.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) for optional analytics, location, notifications, assistant look-ups or communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority — see Section 12.

7.2 If You Are a California or Other U.S. State Resident

Under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws (for example Virginia's VCDPA, Colorado's CPA, and Connecticut's CTDPA), you may have the right to:

  • Know / Access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you and the purposes for collection, as described in Section 2 and Section 3.
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions (e.g. completing a transaction, legal compliance).
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of "sale" or "sharing" — we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of; we do not use dark patterns to obscure this fact.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information — we do not use verification documents or precise geolocation for any purpose beyond verification, safety, and providing the core booking/discovery features you request.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny you goods or services, or provide a different level of service, because you exercised a privacy right.
  • Appeal a denied request, and designate an authorised agent to submit a request on your behalf (subject to identity verification).

To exercise any right in Section 7.1 or 7.2, contact us at admin@pro-host.tech or use the in-app options. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally thirty (30) to forty-five (45) days).

8. Security

We employ industry-standard security measures: HTTPS-only data transmission, Firebase Security Rules limiting data access to authenticated owners, server-side-only enforcement of sensitive fields (role, verification status, payment entitlements), password hashing by Firebase Authentication, app-integrity checks (Firebase App Check), private storage of verification documents, and no storage of payment card data. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach creating a real risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and, where legally required, the competent supervisory authority, without undue delay and in any event within the timeframe required by applicable law (for example 72 hours under GDPR Art. 33 where feasible).

9. Cookies and Tracking on This Website

The ProHost marketing website (pro-host.tech) uses no third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers; see Google's Privacy Policy for how font requests are handled. The site is served by Netlify, which processes technical request data (see Section 5). We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal and equivalent "Do Not Track" browser signals as a valid opt-out request under CCPA/CPRA where applicable, though as noted in Section 5 we do not sell or share personal information in any event.

10. Children's Privacy

ProHost is not directed at persons under 18 years of age (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete the account and associated data promptly.
