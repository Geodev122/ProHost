FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent a yoga studio for one class a week?

Yes. Book a single hour or a weekly slot. After the host accepts, the slot is reserved on each date of your booking.

What does a studio listing show?

Photos, capacity, facilities such as mats, mirrors, sound system or changing rooms, the pricing method and live availability for each room.

Can I photograph or film in a studio?

Ask the host on WhatsApp from the listing. Many studios welcome photo and video shoots outside class hours.

Do I pay ProHost a commission?

No. Booking is free for instructors. You pay the studio directly.
