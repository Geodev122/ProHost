FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a classroom for a whole course term?

Yes. Choose the weekly days and hours and the start date. Once the host accepts, every session date in the term is reserved for you.

How does per-attendee pricing work?

The host sets a price per person, sometimes in tiers by group size. You enter the number of attendees and ProHost shows the total for the whole booking.

What equipment do training rooms have?

Each room lists its capacity and facilities, such as a projector or screen, whiteboard, Wi-Fi and a generator. Ask the host on WhatsApp for anything specific.

How do I find a training room near my trainees?

Open the map in the app, search the area where your trainees are and filter by space type and pricing formula. The map shows what is open today.
