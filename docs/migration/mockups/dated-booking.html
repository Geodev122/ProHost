Booking on real dates

Today the availability sheet lists weekdays ("Mon", "Tue") and their slots, and the app quietly books the next occurrence. In the proposal the professional always picks dates on a calendar first (or a start date for monthly rooms), and the host gets closed dates, a booking window and protection for rooms that already have bookings. Example rooms, names and prices are invented; "today" in these drawings is 14 November 2026.

What changes
Hourly, shift, day rooms
Before: tap a weekday, then a slot; the date is chosen for you.
After: tap one or more dates on a calendar, then the free slots of each date.
Monthly rooms
Before: one "slot" per weekday at the monthly price, starting mid-week.
After: pick a start date and a number of months; the end date and total are shown.
What is taken
Before: a slot booked for a specific date could still look free.
After: every date shows exactly which slots are free, taken or closed, and the server refuses clashes.
Professional · step 1

Pick dates, then slots

One sheet for every room type. The calendar marks days that have free slots; closed, full and past days cannot be tapped. Each chosen date gets its own card.

Professional · monthly rooms

Pick a start date and how many months

Any start date; the booking runs whole months from that day. Dates that would run into another booking or a month the host excluded cannot be picked, and the months stepper stops where availability ends.

Re-book and Edit in My Rentals use this same picker, replacing today's "Next Monday / Custom date" chips and the typed date. The confirmation, My Rentals, the host's request card, WhatsApp text and emails all list the real dates and hours.

Host · creating and editing a room

Closed dates, booking window, and protection for booked rooms

The weekly pattern stays the way a host describes a normal week. Two additions sit under "More options". When a room already has upcoming bookings, editing is guarded.

Checks added when saving a room: every offered hour needs a price above zero; start hour before end hour; monthly end not before start; prices keep their decimals (12.50 stays 12.50); months a host excludes show as unavailable.
