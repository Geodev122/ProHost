# ProHost marketing kit

Ten Instagram videos telling one story in the second person: *You're a… / You run a…* → the problem → *With ProHost /
Now you can / Finally…* → the solution in the app → **Download ProHost** + **the first month of ProHost Premium free
(monthly plan; 50% off the first year on the yearly plan)**. Videos aimed at professionals add "Booking is free · Premium is for listing your space".

## Folders

| Folder | What's inside | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `01-reels-english-9x16/` | 10 Reels, 1080×1920, 16 s · British English voiceover + music + UI sounds | Instagram/Facebook Reels, Stories, TikTok, YouTube Shorts |
| `02-reels-arabic-9x16/` | the same 10 Reels with the Lebanese Arabic voiceover | Lebanese audience |
| `03-landscape-16x9/` | Reel 1 (space owners) re-laid out at 1920×1080, English and Arabic, + cover | YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook feed, website, presentations |
| `04-feed-posts-4x5-music/` | the 10 videos as 4:5 feed posts (1080×1350), music + UI sounds, no voice | Instagram/Facebook feed |
| `05-covers/` | `reels-9x16/` covers, `grid-previews-3x4/` (what the profile grid shows), `feed-posts-4x5/` covers | choose as cover when posting |
| `06-silent-videos-9x16/` | the Reels with no audio | re-editing with your own music or voice |
| `audio/` | `voiceovers-english/`, `voiceovers-arabic/` (full 16 s tracks, single lines, timing report) and `music/` (Lyria tracks) | re-mixing |
| `backgrounds/` | the 13 photo backgrounds (Vertex AI, no people, no brands) | other designs |
| `source/` | animation templates, `render.py`, `mix.py`, `export.py` | regenerating everything |

Every file is numbered the same way in every folder: `01-space-owners`, `02-the-app`, `03-psychologist`,
`04-corporate-trainer`, `05-life-coach`, `06-dietitian`, `07-hotel`, `08-medical-center`, `09-training-center`, `10-yoga-studio`.

**Audio mix:** voiceover on top; the post's own Lyria music is dimmed and dips a further ~4 dB whenever the voice speaks;
UI sounds (whoosh on scene changes, pop on cards, click on taps, chime for a new request, sparkle on send/accept, impact
on the "1 month free" badge) follow the animation. Everything is levelled to -14 LUFS.

**Before posting:** the monthly `free-4-months` offer must be live in Play Console (the videos say "1 month free"; the yearly 50% offer is only the caption/pill). The download
link goes in your bio: **pro-host.tech/ig** (tagged for Instagram; `/fb`, `/wa`, `/li`, `/qr` for other channels).

  "Marketing voiceovers (manual)" with the matching config. Output: `audio/voiceovers-*`.
- **Music:** `scripts/marketing/music.json` → Actions › "Marketing music (manual)" → `audio/music/`.
- **Backgrounds:** `scripts/marketing/backgrounds.json` → Actions › "Marketing backgrounds (manual)" → `backgrounds/`.
- **Videos:** see `source/README.md` (render → mix → export).
- **AI-avatar Reel** (`02-reels-arabic-9x16/12-office-sourcing-avatar.mp4`, office sourcing, first month free via the
  form, then $8.99/month): presenter from `avatar/reference/character_sheet.jpg` (`scripts/marketing/avatar_refs.py`), shots
  in `scripts/marketing/avatar_shots.json` → Actions › "Marketing AI avatar (manual)": `keyframes` (Gemini image), `clips`
  (Veo fast, capped at 48 s per run, cost in `avatar/report.json`), `check` (Gemini transcribes the lip-synced lines) →
  `python source/reel_v3_compose.py` (cuts to the voice, overlay `source/reel_office_v3.html`, score). Shots 1 and 5 are
  Veo's own lip-synced voice, the rest the v2 voice-over. Post it from the Instagram app with **AI info** switched on.


## Instagram grid, posts 4-6 and the DM Story (`social/`)

The live account (@prohost.app) shows three single 4:5 typographic posts in a light / dark / light rhythm (read with Maintenance `igmedia`, images in `live/`).
**Design DNA:** flat navy `#001f4e` or off-white `#fefefc`, the checkmark logo + ProHost wordmark top-left, a huge tightly-tracked bold grotesque headline (Inter ExtraBold, -4 px), one thin orange `#fd631d` rule, one regular-weight line of value, one orange call-to-action block (or full-width orange band on light posts); no photos, no gradients. Captions: a hook, one value paragraph, one call to action (message us / link in bio), three hashtags.

The grid continues **dark, light, dark** (publish order matters: oldest first):

| Post | Theme | Headline | CTA | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | dark | Rent it your way. | DM us LIST to get started. | `social/post11_rent_your_way.html` |
| 12 | light | Add it. Price it. Publish it. | Questions? DM us HOW and we'll walk you through. | `social/post12_add_price_publish.html` |
| 13 | dark | Questions at 2 am? | Send us a DM. | `social/post13_ask_prohost.html` |

Captions and the Story entry are in `social/captions.json`; `python social/render_posts.py` renders the PNG/JPEG into `social/out/` and checks the safe margins. The agent answers a DM or comment with LIST or HOW through the normal AI flow (no extra rules).

**Story (`source/story_dm_agent.html`, 18 s, 1080x1920):** hook, a DM thread showing the agent's real opening flow, languages and topics, end card "Send us a DM." with the arrow towards Instagram's *Send message* bar (top 250 px / bottom 340 px kept free). British English voice-over (`scripts/marketing/voiceover_en.json`, `story_dm_agent`, voice Sulafat), Lyria music (`scripts/marketing/music.json`), UI sounds, mixed with `LENGTH=18 VOICE=audio/voiceovers-english python mix.py story_dm_agent` (renderer: `VIEWPORT=1080x1920 python render.py story_dm_agent.html 18 30`). -14 LUFS.

**Publishing:** files are served from `public/social/` (Firebase Hosting) and published with Maintenance `igpublish` (code = `post11`, `post12`, `post13`, `story`; empty = dry-run plan; never publishes an item twice, `ig_stats/published`). The posts avoid prices and offers on purpose; any future offer text must match `functions/src/billing/offerClaims.ts`.
