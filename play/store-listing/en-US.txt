TITLE:
ProHost: Rooms by the Hour

SHORT:
Book or rent out clinic rooms, offices, studios & meeting rooms in Lebanon.

FULL:
ProHost is the workspace rental app for Lebanon. Professionals book clinic rooms, therapy rooms, offices, training rooms, studios and meeting rooms by the hour, shift, day, month or per attendee. Space owners turn empty rooms into steady income.

FOR PROFESSIONALS: BOOK EXACTLY THE TIME YOU NEED
Psychologists who see clients two evenings a week. Doctors who need a clinic every Tuesday morning. Trainers with 20 people on Saturday. Coaches, dietitians, physiotherapists, consultants and freelancers. ProHost lets you book the room you need, only for the time you need it, without a long lease.

• Search clinics, therapy rooms, offices, studios, classrooms and meeting rooms on a live map of Beirut and Lebanon
• Filter by space type, room type and pricing: hourly, shift, daily, monthly or per attendee
• See every room's photos, capacity, facilities and live availability before you book
• Pick your days and hours and see the total before you send your request
• Message the host on WhatsApp with one tap
• Track every request and booking in My Rentals, mark payments and add payment reminders to your calendar
• Save your favourite spaces
• Free to download and free to book

FOR SPACE OWNERS: FILL THE HOURS YOUR ROOMS SIT EMPTY
Hotels with meeting rooms that are empty on weekdays. Medical centers and polyclinics with clinic rooms free in the afternoons. Training centers, institutes and schools with classrooms free at night. Yoga, pilates and dance studios. Coworking spaces, business centers and office owners.

• List your space and every room in it, each with its own photos, schedule and pricing
• Rent by the hour, shift, day, month or per attendee
• Receive requests from professionals with a verified phone number and a profile photo
• Accept or decline in one tap; accepted bookings lock their slots, so there are no double bookings
• See the week's occupancy and this month's revenue for every room
• Change prices any time, now or from the next term; ProHost notifies your tenants for you
• No commission on your bookings

PROHOST PREMIUM
Publishing and managing listings is part of ProHost Premium, a monthly or yearly subscription billed by Google Play. Booking a space is always free. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

WHERE
Beirut (Achrafieh, Hamra, Verdun, Badaro, Mar Mikhael, Gemmayzeh, Downtown), Mount Lebanon (Jounieh, Jdeideh, Dbayeh, Antelias, Baabda, Hazmieh, Sin El Fil), Byblos, Tripoli, Saida, Tyre, Zahle, Nabatieh and across Lebanon.
